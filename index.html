Why we built it

A recipe kept in memory is one phone call away from being lost.

  • No cards. No measurements.
  • Explained once at the stove, again over the phone.
  • Sitting in a voice note nobody plays back.

How it works

From what was said to a recipe you keep

01 · Record it

Hit record while they talk

  • At the stove or over the phone
  • Up to 10 minutes, or choose a voice note
  • Mixed Urdu, Hindi, Punjabi, and English
  • Or paste a YouTube, recipe site, or Instagram link

02 · Keep it

Andaza stays andaza

  • Ingredients and steps, pulled out for you
  • Vague amounts stay vague. No guessed numbers.
  • What they said stays under each ingredient
  • Missing items go to a shopping list in one tap

03 · Share it

One family, one cookbook

  • Private to the people you invite
  • Invite with a link or a code
  • Joining is free
  • Starting one needs Samaan Pro

Heads-up on the Community book. Recipes from a recording or pasted words also show there for signed-in cooks. Delete the recipe to take it down. Recipes from links stay private.
