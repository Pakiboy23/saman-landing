Why we built it

A recipe kept in memory is one phone call away from being lost.

Desi recipes travel by word and by hand. No cards, no measurements, nothing written down. A dish learned standing next to someone, explained again over the phone the first time you try it alone, sitting in a voice note nobody goes back to.

Samaan Pantry is for writing those down while there is still someone to ask.

It is not a grocery delivery service, a price-comparison tool, or inventory software for a business.

How it works

From what was said to a recipe you keep

01 · Record it

Hit record while someone talks

Tap Capture a recipe and record someone explaining the dish, on the phone or standing at the stove. You can also type or paste a transcript, or a message that was typed out. Typed or pasted text can mix Urdu, Hindi, Punjabi, and English. You can still paste a link to a website or YouTube video instead.

02 · Keep it

Andaza stays andaza

Samaan Pantry pulls out the ingredients and steps, and leaves a little andaza as andaza instead of guessing a number nobody said. What was actually said stays with the recipe, so you can always go back to those words.

03 · Family book

It goes in the shared family book

A recipe made from a transcript or a recording publishes to one shared family book. Signed-in users can read it. Short on ingredients? Send them to a shopping list and check them off as you buy them.

Coming soon: family cookbooks. Create a private family book, invite family by link or code, and everyone keeps the recipes. No date yet. See the changelog.

Pantry and lists work without an account. Recipe extraction needs a signed-in account. Sign in with email when you're ready to save recipes to the family book. Free includes 5 recipe extractions for life and Samaan Pro is unlimited. The first time, the app asks before sending your recipe to Anthropic's AI to read it. Check the ingredients and amounts before you shop or cook. The privacy policy says what is collected.

Home · Pantry · Lists · Recipes

The kitchen around the recipe

Know what's in the pantry

From atta and daal to the last jar of ghee, keep your staples in view. Scan a barcode instead of typing every staple in by hand, or add an item yourself. Set the minimum you like to keep on hand, and see what's running low on Home before your next shop.

Keep the next shop in order

Add what you need to a shopping list, whether it came off a recipe or out of your head. Check off an item as you buy it, and its matching pantry item updates too.

Start before you sign up

Try pantry and lists without an account. Recipe extraction needs a signed-in account. When you sign in with email, your kitchen syncs to your account, and what you added before your first sign-in comes along.
