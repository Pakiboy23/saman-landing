Why we built it

A recipe kept in memory is one phone call away from being lost.

Desi recipes travel by word and by hand. No cards, no measurements, nothing written down. A dish learned standing next to someone, explained again over the phone the first time you try it alone, sitting in a voice note nobody goes back to.

Samaan Pantry is for writing those down while there is still someone to ask.

How it works

From what she said to a recipe you keep

01 · Paste it

Paste what was said

Tap Capture a recipe and paste the words in: a transcript of her voice note, the message she typed out at midnight, or a link to a website or YouTube video she sent. A mix of English and Urdu, Hindi, or Punjabi is expected, not a problem. If a link has no recipe in it, the app says so.

02 · Keep it

Andaza stays andaza

Samaan Pantry pulls out the ingredients and steps, and leaves a little andaza as andaza instead of guessing a number nobody said. Your original text stays with the recipe, so you can always read it back the way she told it.

03 · Pass it on

Cook from it, then send it

Pick the ingredients you're short on and add them to a shopping list. Check them off as you buy them and your pantry count follows along. From the recipe page, share the recipe with whoever asks you for it next.

Recipe extraction needs a signed-in account and allows 5 extractions a day. The first time, the app asks before sending your recipe text to Anthropic's AI to read it. Check the ingredients and amounts before you shop or cook.

Home · Pantry · Lists · Recipes

The kitchen around the recipe

Know what's in the pantry

From atta and daal to the last jar of ghee, keep your staples in view. Scan a barcode or add an item yourself, set the minimum you like to keep on hand, and see what's running low on Home before your next shop.

Keep the next shop in order

Add what you need to a shopping list, whether it came off a recipe or out of your head. Check off an item as you buy it, and its matching pantry item updates too.

Start before you sign up

Try it without an account. When you sign in with email, your kitchen syncs to your account, and what you added before your first sign-in comes along.

Start free. Add more when you need it.
