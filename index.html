A place for the everyday essentials

Know what's in the pantry

From atta and daal to the last jar of ghee, keep your staples in view. Scan a barcode or add an item yourself, set the minimum you like to keep on hand, and see what's running low on Home before your next shop.

Keep the next shop in order

Add what you need to a shopping list. Check off an item as you buy it, and its matching pantry item updates too.

Keep the recipes you come back to

Paste a recipe the way it was written or said, including recipes in a mix of English and Urdu, Hindi, or Punjabi. Samaan Pantry pulls out the ingredients and steps, leaves a little andaza as andaza instead of guessing a number, and keeps your original text with the recipe. Pick the ingredients you need and add them to a shopping list.

Start before you sign up

Try it without an account. When you sign in with email, your kitchen syncs to your account, and what you added before your first sign-in comes along.

Recipe extraction needs a signed-in account and allows 5 extractions a day. The first time, the app asks before sending your recipe text to Anthropic's AI to read it. Check the ingredients and amounts before you shop or cook.

Start free. Add more when you need it.

Room for more with Saman Pro

Unlimited pantry items and shopping lists.

  • ✓
    Unlimited pantry items Track every staple in the kitchen.
  • ✓
    Unlimited shopping lists Keep weekly, festival, and bulk lists organized.

Free includes up to 30 pantry items and 1 shopping list from the Lists tab. Lists made from a recipe don't count toward that. Recipe extraction is 5 a day on Free and Saman Pro.
