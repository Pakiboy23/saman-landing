Why it matters

Every recipe held in memory
is one phone call away
from being lost.

South Asian recipes travel through generations by word and hand. No recipe cards. No measurements. A dish learned by watching, explained over a phone call, tucked away in a voice note nobody ever goes back to.

Samaan Pantry was built to rescue those recipes while there is still time to ask.
