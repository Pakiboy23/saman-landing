Recipe first, then the restock

Most kitchen apps start with inventory. The harder problem is the recipe.

It lives in a call, a voice note, a paragraph of andaza. Mixed languages. No recipe card. Then you still have to shop, and you still have to know what is already in the house.

Samaan Pantry starts with capturing that recipe, then connects it to a shopping list and the pantry so you can make the dish again.

Capture · Extract · Shop · Stock

From the way it was told, to what you need tonight

Capture it as it was told

Record a recipe in the app, choose an audio file, paste a link from a website or YouTube, or paste the words, including a mix of English and Urdu, Hindi, or Punjabi. Recordings are transcribed on your iPhone. The audio stays on the phone.

If a link has no recipe, the app says so.

Leave andaza as andaza

Samaan Pantry pulls out the ingredients and steps, keeps a little andaza as andaza instead of guessing a number, and stores the original with the recipe. From the recipe page, share any saved recipe with friends and family.

Shop from the recipe

Pick the ingredients you need and add them to a shopping list. Check an item off as you buy it, and its matching pantry item updates too.

Keep the pantry underneath

From atta and daal to the last jar of ghee, keep staples in view. Scan a barcode or add an item yourself, set the minimum you like to keep on hand, and see what's running low on Home before the next shop.

You can try the pantry and lists without an account. Recipe extraction needs a signed-in account and allows 5 extractions a day. The first time, the app asks before sending your recipe text to Anthropic's AI to read it. Check the ingredients and amounts before you shop or cook.

Start free. Add more when you need it.
