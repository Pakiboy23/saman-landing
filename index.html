What Samaan Pantry does

Pantry

See atta, daal, ghee, and the masalas in one place. Scan a barcode or add the item yourself. When the quantity reaches the low mark, it shows under Running low. The app does not send a notification you did not ask for.

Lists

Keep a running shopping list. Check an item off as you shop and the matching pantry item restocks. You still shop wherever you already shop.

Capture a recipe

Paste a link from a website, YouTube, or Instagram, or paste the words — code-switched, andaza and all. Samaan Pantry tries to pull the recipe in and keeps the phrase that was actually said. It does not invent a number. Review it before you shop or cook.

Share a recipe

From a saved recipe, share the ingredients and the steps. The share leaves your phone as text, and as a card when the phone can draw one.

You can start without an account. Sign in when you want sync, recipe extract, or to delete an account.
