Support

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Samaan Pantry is the iPhone app for one desi kitchen: pantry, lists, and the recipes you actually cook. If something is broken, missing, or unclear, email haaris@samantechnologies.com. A person reads it.

In the app

Add what you have. Keep a running list of what you need. Capture a recipe, then shop.

  • Pantry — add an item or scan a barcode. A low mark is the quantity that counts as running out. Running low shows on Home. The app does not send a notification.
  • Lists — check an item off and the matching pantry item restocks. You shop wherever you already shop.
  • Capture a recipe — paste a link from a website, YouTube, or Instagram and the app tries to pull the recipe in. Or paste the words. Review the result before you shop or cook. Extract is 5 a day for everyone.
  • Share a recipe — from a saved recipe, share the ingredients and steps.
  • Saman Pro — more than 30 pantry items, and extra lists you add from the Lists tab. Extract stays 5 a day.

Account

  • Sign in with the email you used to create the account.
  • Forgot password is on the sign-in screen. We email a reset link. Check spam if it does not arrive in a minute.
  • Delete your account from Settings inside the app. That removes your pantry data. You can also email us and we will do it within 30 days.

Sync and devices

Samaan Pantry is iPhone-only. Your pantry syncs to the signed-in account, so a reinstall on the same Apple ID should come back after the first sync. If a deleted item returns, force-quit and open the app once more so the delete can finish uploading.

Subscriptions

Paid features go through Apple. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Restore Purchases is in the app paywall. Billing questions that Apple owns, Apple has to answer.

Privacy

What we collect, who we share it with, and how to export it lives on the privacy policy.
