Support

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Need help with Samaan Pantry? Email haaris@samaan.tech with what's happening and we'll help you work through it. A person reads it.

Using the app

  • Pantry. Add an item yourself or scan its barcode. Set a minimum for each item. When the count reaches it, the item shows under Running low on Home. Samaan Pantry doesn't send push notifications.
  • Shopping lists. Check off an item as you buy it and the matching pantry item's count goes up. Uncheck it and the count goes back down.
  • Recipes. Tap Capture a recipe on Home. Record the recipe as it's being explained to you, or paste the recipe text or a link from a website or YouTube, then tap Extract Recipe. If a link has no recipe, the app says so. Check the ingredients and amounts, choose the ones you need, and tap Add to Shopping List. That saves the recipe and makes a shopping list with its name.
  • Sharing a recipe. Open a saved recipe and share it with friends and family from the recipe page. A recipe made from a transcript or a recording also publishes to one shared family book that signed-in users can read.
  • Recipe extraction limits. Extraction needs a signed-in account, whether you recorded the recipe or pasted it. New accounts get 5 recipe extractions for life and Samaan Pro is unlimited. The app tells you when you've used up the free extractions.
  • Samaan Pro. Unlimited recipe extractions, and you keep every recipe you save.

Coming soon: family cookbooks. Create a private family book, invite family by link or code, and everyone keeps the recipes. No date yet. See the changelog.

Account

  • Sign in with the email and password you used to create your account.
  • Forgot password? is on the sign-in screen. We'll email you a reset link. If it doesn't arrive in a minute, check your spam folder.
  • Delete Account is in Settings inside the app. It removes your account, pantry, lists, and recipes from our servers. You can also email us and we'll do it within 30 days.

Sync and devices

Samaan Pantry is for iPhone with iOS 17 or later. You can use pantry and lists without an account, and what you add stays on that iPhone. Recipe extraction needs a signed-in account. When you sign in, your kitchen syncs to your Samaan Pantry account. The first time you sign in, what you added before comes along.

To bring your kitchen back after reinstalling, or on a new iPhone, sign in with the same email and let it sync. Settings has Sync now if you want to sync right away. Signing out clears your kitchen from that iPhone until you sign in again.

Subscriptions

Buy Samaan Pro in the app. Restore Purchases is in Settings and on the upgrade screen. Manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone's subscription settings. For billing or refund help, contact Apple Support.
