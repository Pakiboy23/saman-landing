Changelog

Current as of 21 November 2026 · version 1.7

This page is the dated record of what Samaan Pantry does today.

Current

  • Record a recipe. Hit record while someone talks you through a dish, for up to 10 minutes, or choose a voice note or audio file you already have. Recordings are turned into text on our server.
  • Mixed languages. Recordings and typed text can mix Urdu, Hindi, Punjabi, and English. The transcript is kept with the recipe.
  • Links and typed recipes. Paste a YouTube video, a recipe site, or an Instagram caption, or type it the way they say it.
  • Andaza. Vague amounts stay vague. No number gets guessed.
  • Family cookbooks. Start a private family cookbook and invite people with a link or a code. Only members see it. Starting one needs Samaan Pro. Joining is free.
  • The Community book. A recipe made from a recording or pasted words is added there for signed-in cooks to read. Deleting the recipe takes it down. Recipes from links stay private unless you add them.
  • Pantry and lists. Barcode scan, Running low on Home, and shopping lists that update the pantry. They work without an account.
  • Free. New accounts get 5 recipe extractions for life. No card, no trial clock.
  • Samaan Pro. Unlimited recipe extractions and starting family cookbooks. $7.99 a month or $59.99 a year.
  • iPhone. iOS 17 or later.

Releases

  • 1.7, 18 November 2026. Recordings and audio files are transcribed on our server, so long, mixed-language recipes come through in full. Plus new dark and tinted app icons.
  • 1.6. Private family cookbooks you invite people into. Unlimited extractions on Samaan Pro. A quieter first launch.

Android

Samaan Pantry is an iPhone app. There is no Android version, and there is no ship date. If you want a note when that changes, email haaris@samaan.tech.
