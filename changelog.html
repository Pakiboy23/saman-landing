Changelog

Current as of 14 October 2026

This page is the dated record of what Samaan Pantry does today. Family cookbooks are coming soon, and there is no ship date for them yet.

Current

  • Recipe capture. Hit record while someone talks you through a dish, or type it the way they say it. You can also paste a link to a website or a YouTube video.
  • Typed text. Typed or pasted text can mix Urdu, Hindi, Punjabi, and English. A voice recording is kept with the recipe. The app does not claim to understand those languages in the recording itself.
  • Andaza. A little andaza stays andaza, instead of guessing a number nobody said.
  • One shared family book. A recipe made from a transcript or a recording publishes to one shared family book. Signed-in users can read it.
  • Pantry and lists. Pantry and shopping lists work without an account. Recipe extraction needs a signed-in account.
  • Free. New accounts get 5 recipe extractions for life. No card, no trial clock.
  • Samaan Pro. Unlimited recipe extractions. $7.99 a month or $59.99 a year.
  • iPhone. iOS 17 or later.

Coming soon

Family cookbooks. Create a private family book, invite family by link or code, and everyone keeps the recipes. No date yet.

Android

Samaan Pantry is an iPhone app. There is no Android version, and there is no ship date. If you want a note when that changes, email haaris@samaan.tech.
