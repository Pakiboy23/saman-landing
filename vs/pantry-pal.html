What each one is for

Samaan Pantry. A recipe capture app for desi kitchens. Record someone explaining the dish, or type it the way they say it. Typed or pasted text can mix Urdu, Hindi, Punjabi, and English. You can still paste a link to a website or a YouTube video. A little andaza stays andaza instead of guessing a number nobody said. There is one shared family book today. Per-family cookbooks are coming soon, with no date yet.

Pantry Pal. Its site says you paste a link, photograph a cookbook page, or hand it a video, then cook from what is already on your shelves. Checked 14 October 2026.

Price

Samaan Pantry. Free is $0, with 5 recipe extractions for life. No card, no trial clock. Samaan Pro is unlimited extractions, $7.99 a month or $59.99 a year. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Pantry Pal. Its pricing page lists Pro at $7.99 a month or $59.99 a year. It describes a 30-day trial, then 5 recipe imports a month on the free plan. Checked 14 October 2026.

Account

Samaan Pantry. Pantry and lists work without an account. Recipe extraction needs a signed-in account. Sign in with email when you're ready to save recipes to the family book.

Phone

Samaan Pantry. iPhone with iOS 17 or later. No Android app, and no ship date. See the changelog.
