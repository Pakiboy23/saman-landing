Samaan Pantry and Pantry Pal

Checked 14 October 2026

Hit record while someone talks you through it. Andaza stays andaza. That is what Samaan Pantry is for. Pantry Pal imports a link, a cookbook photo, or a video, and cooks from what you already have.

What each one is for

Samaan Pantry. A recipe capture app for desi kitchens. Hit record while someone explains the dish, at the stove or over the phone, for up to ten minutes, or choose a voice note. Recordings and typed text can mix Urdu, Hindi, Punjabi, and English. You can also paste a YouTube video, a recipe site, or an Instagram caption. Andaza stays andaza instead of guessing a number nobody said. Private family cookbooks sit around it, with a pantry and shopping lists.

Pantry Pal. Its site says you paste a link, photograph a cookbook page, or hand it a video, then cook from what is already on your shelves. Checked 14 October 2026.

Side by side

How a recipe gets in

Samaan Pantry. Hit record while someone talks you through it, for up to ten minutes, or choose a voice note. Paste a YouTube video, a recipe site, or an Instagram caption, or type it the way they say it.

Pantry Pal. Link, cookbook photo, and video imports, as its site describes them.

Andaza

Samaan Pantry. Andaza stays andaza.

Pantry Pal. Built around imports and cooking from what you have. It is not built around leaving a spoken andaza as andaza.

Price

Samaan Pantry. Free is $0. New accounts get 5 recipe extractions for life. No card, no trial clock. Samaan Pro is unlimited extractions, $7.99 a month or $59.99 a year. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Pantry Pal. Its pricing page lists Pro at $7.99 a month or $59.99 a year. It describes a 30-day trial, then 5 recipe imports a month on the free plan. Checked 14 October 2026.

Account

Samaan Pantry. Pantry, lists, and recipes work without an account. Recipe extraction and family cookbooks need a signed-in account.

Phone

Samaan Pantry. iPhone with iOS 17 or later. No Android app, and no ship date. See the changelog.

Pantry Pal. Its site says iOS and Android land later this year. Checked 14 October 2026.

What do they cost?

Is there an Android app?
